The beginning

The day we met

The first time I saw you was in real life. The moment I saw you, I found myself looking at you. There was something about you that caught my attention. I was curious, and I kept wondering, “Why did she look at me?” It was just a small moment. Nothing seemed special about it at the time. But somehow, that one little moment became the beginning of everything. And now, when I look back, it makes me smile knowing that one simple moment has brought us this far. ❤️

💬
First conversations

When talking became a habit

The first time you came into my life was through a follow from an account I didn’t recognize. I was curious about who you were, so I went to check your profile. After that, I simply texted you, “Hi.” I got a reply from the other side. But honestly, I never expected that one little reply to change my life. That day, you told me who you were, although I don’t think I really knew much about you at the time. Then, somehow, we ended up talking for the whole next day. It was only after that that I really started to know you. And honestly, I thought you probably wouldn’t talk to me after that. But you did. You kept talking to me, and our conversations just continued. Maybe that was the real beginning of us. It all started with one follow, one “Hi,” and one reply. And look how far that little beginning has brought us. ❤️

😊
A special moment

The memory I'll always keep

Our last meet was really special to me. I remember thinking that I never wanted to forget that moment. I don’t even know how that one hour passed so quickly. But what made it so special was how comfortable I felt with you. I was just sitting there, talking to you, and somehow everything felt so natural. I don’t really know how to explain it. I just know that, for that one hour, being with you felt incredibly comfortable and peaceful. Maybe that’s what made that moment so special to me. ❤️
